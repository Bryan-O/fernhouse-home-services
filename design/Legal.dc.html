This policy explains what information Fernhouse collects when you visit our website or book a cleaning, how we use it, and the choices you have. Fernhouse provides house cleaning and pet waste removal in Atlanta, Georgia and surrounding suburbs.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Your name, mobile phone number and email address.
  • Your home’s address, ZIP code, size, and any access instructions, pet notes or special requests you add.
  • Messages you send us, and your feedback after a clean.

Payment information

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card number goes directly to Stripe — Fernhouse never sees or stores it. We receive a confirmation of payment and the last four digits of your card.

Information collected automatically

When you visit our site we collect basic device and usage information (such as browser type, pages viewed and referring site), and the advertising details in the link you arrived from (such as campaign tags and click identifiers from Facebook, Instagram or Google).

How we use your information

  • To schedule, complete and support your cleanings, including recurring plans.
  • To give your assigned cleaning team the details they need for your visit — your address, access instructions, pets and preferences.
  • To send booking confirmations, reminders and updates by text and email.
  • To process payments, refunds and re-cleans.
  • To understand which ads and pages work, improve our service, and prevent fraud.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Text messages

When you book or ask for a quote, you agree to get texts from Fernhouse about that booking or quote: confirmations, reminders, arrival and visit updates (including gate photos for yard service), and receipts. Message frequency varies. We send marketing texts — no more than 2 a month — only if you tick the separate, optional box to receive them. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts at any time, or HELP for help.

We don’t share your mobile number or text-message consent with anyone for their own marketing. Mobile information is never sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We share it only with the texting provider that delivers our messages.

Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar tools from Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to measure site traffic and the results of our ads. You can limit this through your browser’s cookie settings, the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, and your Meta ad preferences.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With your assigned cleaning team, limited to what they need to clean your home.
  • With companies that help us run Fernhouse — payments (Stripe), text messaging, email, website hosting and analytics — under agreements that limit their use of it.
  • When required by law, or to protect the safety and rights of our customers, our team or the public.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records while you’re a customer and for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Access instructions, such as door and garage codes, are removed from our systems when you end your service.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. Access instructions are visible only to the team assigned to your visit. No system is perfectly secure, so please tell us right away if you think your information has been misused.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing fernhousehomeservices@gmail.com. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails with the link in any email.

Children

Our website and services are for adults. We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page. Questions? Email fernhousehomeservices@gmail.com or call (678) 439-6213.

These terms apply when you book a cleaning with Fernhouse through our website or by phone. By booking, you agree to them. Please read them — they’re written to be short and plain.

Booking and payment

  • You book and pay Fernhouse online through our secure checkout, powered by Stripe. You pay Fernhouse, never your cleaner directly.
  • Your first visit is charged when you book. For recurring plans, each later visit is charged to your card on file on the day of the visit.
  • Prices include all supplies and equipment. There are no hidden fees.

Prices

Our prices are starting prices based on the bedrooms and bathrooms you choose, for a home in normal condition. If your home is much larger than booked, or needs far more work than a typical clean, your team will contact us before starting. We’ll call you to agree on an adjusted price or reschedule — we never add charges without asking.

First-clean offer

New customers get 20% off their first booking. One offer per household. It can’t be combined with other offers or exchanged for cash.

Recurring plans

Weekly and biweekly plans have no contract. You can pause, reschedule or cancel your plan anytime from your confirmation link, subject to the 24-hour rule below.

Cancellations and rescheduling

  • Reschedule or cancel free up to 24 hours before your visit.
  • Changes made inside 24 hours carry a $50 late-cancellation fee, because your team has already been scheduled.
  • If your team arrives during your window and can’t get in, the visit counts as a late cancellation.
  • If we ever need to reschedule your visit, there’s no charge, and we’ll offer you the nearest open slot.

Re-clean guarantee

If anything we cleaned isn’t right, tell us within 24 hours of your visit — photos help. We’ll send a team back to re-clean those areas, free. If a re-clean doesn’t fix the problem, we’ll work with you on a fair refund.

Pet waste removal

  • We visit on your chosen route day and check the whole yard. Waste is bagged and hauled away.
  • Each visit is charged to your card on file after it’s done. If waste has built up, your first visit is an initial yard cleanup, priced by the yard condition you pick when you book; after that you pay the per-visit price.
  • Yard condition: we rely on the condition you pick. If your yard needs more cleanup than you picked, we’ll text you before your first visit is charged at the higher rate, and you can cancel instead without a fee.
  • Please give us gate access — a code, a note, or an unlocked gate. If we can’t get in or a dog makes the yard unsafe to enter, we’ll text you and arrange another time; a missed visit due to no access is charged as a late cancellation.
  • Re-scoop guarantee: if we missed something, tell us within 24 hours of your visit and we’ll come back free.
  • We don’t offer flea, tick or any pesticide treatments.

Access, safety and pets

  • Please give us accurate access instructions and make sure your team can get in during your arrival window.
  • Let us know about pets when you book, and secure any pet that may not be comfortable around visitors.
  • Please put away cash, jewelry and other valuables before your visit.
  • Your team may skip any task they consider unsafe, such as work at heights beyond a step stool, moving heavy furniture, or cleaning bodily fluids, mold or pest waste.

Damage and claims

Every pro we send carries their own bond and liability insurance, which we verify before they’re booked. If something is damaged during a visit, tell us within 48 hours with photos. We’ll handle the claim with you and the pro’s insurer, and work to get it repaired, replaced or reimbursed. We aren’t responsible for normal wear, pre-existing damage, or items that were improperly installed or secured.

Hiring your team directly

Our guarantees, backup coverage and insurance apply only to cleanings booked through Fernhouse. During your service and for 12 months after your last visit, you agree not to hire any member of a team Fernhouse sends to your home for cleaning outside of Fernhouse. If you do, a $1,000 placement fee applies.

Liability and governing law

To the extent the law allows, Fernhouse’s total liability for any claim related to a cleaning is limited to the amount you paid for that cleaning, except for damage covered under the section above. These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Georgia.
